About the Game
Barely Out Alive is a small atmospheric horror game focused on exploration, tension, and survival. The player finds themselves in a dark and stormy environment where they must complete objectives while avoiding a dangerous hunter that stalks the area.
The game focuses on creating a tense atmosphere through environmental design, weather effects, and limited player safety. Players must carefully explore their surroundings while remaining aware of the threat that may be nearby.
This version of the game represents a beta build, showcasing the core mechanics and direction of the project.
Gameplay
Players explore the environment surrounding a house while completing a series of objectives. While doing so, they must avoid a hostile hunter that will end the game if the player is caught.
The goal is to complete tasks while surviving long enough to escape the threat.
Currently, the beta version does not include a final win condition, meaning the game will eventually result in the player being caught. This version focuses on demonstrating the core systems and atmosphere.

Controls
W / A / S / D – Move
Mouse – Look around
E – Interact with objects
ESC – Pause menu 

Structured Gameplay Loop: A fully functional 5-10 minute survival experience with a progressive objective system (including tasks like mopping, collecting photos, dumping trash, gathering bones, and placing offerings on an altar).
Immersive Atmosphere & Audio: Detailed environment design featuring rain/weather effects, paired with tension-building audio mechanics like dynamic heartbeats and footsteps.

Active Enemy Threat: A Hunter enemy that actively patrols the area, forcing the player to stay alert while completing chores.
Atmospheric Climax:The environment and tension escalate as the player progresses through their objectives towards the final sequence.

Interactive Environment: Clear visual feedback for objectives (such as item glowing) to help guide the player through their tasks.

Core Systems: Functional pause menu and UI elements to support the player experience.
Known Issues / Limitations As this is an early beta version, some systems are still being developed or improved. Current limitations include:

Character and enemy animations are still a work in progress and being refined.
Gameplay systems and enemy difficulty are still actively being balanced.
Minor interaction quirks may occur if tasks are approached out of the expected sequence.
Future Development
Planned improvements for the final version include:

Improved and smoother animations for both the player character and enemies.
Additional gameplay threats and expanded AI behaviors to increase the challenge.
Further environmental polish, lighting improvements, and detailing.
Expanded narrative elements to flesh out the game's lore.
Development Notes
Barely Out Alive was developed as part of a coursework project. During development, several new skills were learned and applied, including state-driven gameplay programming, dynamic audio integration, AI pathfinding, and environment design. The project was developed independently, requiring full responsibility for all aspects of the game from concept to execution.
